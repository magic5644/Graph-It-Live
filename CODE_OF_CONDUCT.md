# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Graph-It-Live to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming a mistake was intentional.
- Help newcomers: a clear reproduction and a kind answer cost little.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory language.
- Public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated pressure on maintainers for a decision or a release date.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form only when it also involves a security problem. Otherwise, contact the maintainer [@magic5644](https://github.com/magic5644) through the contact options of their GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue about a person's behavior.

Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block accounts that break this Code of Conduct.
